# Image prompts for vakt-spillet
Style for all: pixel art, dark hospital setting, very dark navy background (#0a0f14),
teal (#0a9396) and amber (#ee9b00) accent colors, moody cinematic lighting, 16:9 ratio, 600x300px.

---

## 2024 scenarios (tense, frustrating, messy)

### 2024-login.png
Pixel art of a tired nurse in dark blue scrubs sitting at a hospital workstation at night.
Two monitors glow harshly. Post-it notes cover the screen edge. A password error dialog
is visible on one screen. The other screen shows a login page. Dim overhead light.
Dark navy background. Teal monitor glow. Amber warning on screen. Mood: frustrated, stuck.

### 2024-patient.png
Pixel art of a nurse in a dark hospital corridor at night, holding a phone to her ear,
looking stressed. A patient room door is visible behind her, slightly ajar with warm light.
The nurse's face shows concern. She holds a paper folder in her other hand.
Dark navy tones, amber hallway lighting, teal accent on scrubs. Mood: waiting, uncertain.

### 2024-dobbel.png
Pixel art of a cluttered nurse station at night. Three different software windows open on
a monitor, all showing slightly different patient data. A handwritten sticky note on the desk.
A ringing phone icon visible. Coffee cup half empty. The nurse's hands are on the keyboard,
mid-typing. Dark background, harsh screen glow, amber warning indicators. Mood: overloaded.

### 2024-alarm.png
Pixel art of a hospital corridor at night, alarm light flashing red on the wall.
A nurse running toward a room. Another nurse at a desk looking at a phone, confused.
Two separate screens visible with different interfaces — they don't match.
Dark navy, red alarm glow, teal accent lighting. Mood: reactive, chaotic.

### 2024-skifte.png
Pixel art of two nurses in a handover at 6am. One is exhausted, gesturing from memory.
The other takes handwritten notes. A stack of paper and sticky notes between them.
A clock on the wall shows 05:45. Pale morning light through a small window.
Dark navy tones, amber desk lamp, teal scrubs. Mood: drained, incomplete.

## 2030 scenarios (calm, smooth, in control)

### 2030-login.png
Pixel art of a confident nurse at a clean, modern hospital workstation at night.
One monitor, one clean interface showing a unified dashboard — patient list, schedule,
medication all visible. No clutter. No sticky notes. A subtle teal glow from the screen.
Dark navy background, teal UI elements, soft amber accent light overhead. Mood: calm, ready.

### 2030-patient.png
Pixel art of a nurse in a patient room at night, looking at a tablet.
The screen shows a complete patient profile — allergy info, medication history.
The patient in the bed looks peaceful, not waiting. Clean room, soft teal lighting from device.
Dark navy background, warm patient room light, teal screen glow. Mood: safe, efficient.

### 2030-oppdatering.png
Pixel art of a nurse at a station making a single tap on a tablet. On the screen, 
multiple small icons light up green — showing automatic sync to different systems.
A satisfied expression. Everything works. No paper. No second screen.
Dark navy, teal success indicators, clean UI. Mood: effortless, done.

### 2030-alarm.png
Pixel art of a calm hospital corridor at night. An alarm light glows softly amber —
not panic, just notification. A nurse checks a tablet calmly. On the screen, a message thread
with a doctor — quick response visible. The room door is open, situation handled.
Dark navy, amber glow, teal interface. Mood: in control, coordinated.

### 2030-skifte.png
Pixel art of two nurses in a handover at 6am. One hands a tablet to the other — a clean,
complete shift summary visible on screen. Both look relaxed. The outgoing nurse has her
jacket on, ready to leave. Morning light through the window. No paper anywhere.
Dark navy, teal tablet screen, warm morning light. Mood: complete, peaceful, done.
